Install

On a phone: open this page in Safari/Chrome → Share → “Add to Home Screen”. It then works offline and full-screen. Audio keeps playing with the screen off on Android; on iOS keep the app in the foreground or use the lock-screen player controls.

Not a medical device. Audio entrainment is generally regarded as safe, but stop if you feel unwell. If you have epilepsy, a seizure history, a pacemaker, or are pregnant, check with a clinician first. Never use while driving.
